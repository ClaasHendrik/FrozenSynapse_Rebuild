# Cold Synapse: look notes from the owner's in-game reference pictures (15 screenshots, not stored in the repo)

What the references show, to be matched in the Cold Synapse board (NOT the new game, which gets its own graphics):

* **Floor:** deep blue, almost black, with large soft light pools / shafts of light that fall through openings and
  spread across the floor (they follow line of sight). Unlit areas are nearly black.
* **Walls:** glowing blue extruded blocks with bright edges; full walls dark-blue, half-height cover (boxes, tables) a
  lighter cyan-blue. Floating cubes drift outside and inside the arena as debris. The playfield sits in a bordered frame.
* **Units:** abstract human silhouettes, ONE solid colour (green / red / yellow), a bit hunched with a visible weapon,
  facing the aim direction. A tiny HUD above each: ammo pips and a unit number. Dead units become a splatter of the team colour.
* **Orders:** a white polyline with solid GREEN SQUARE waypoints; a crosshair-in-circle aim marker with a dashed white
  line to it; a small running-man icon for movement type, small clock icon for wait; a dashed arrow for a pending
  direction; a rotation widget (arrows) around the selected unit/waypoint.
* **Interface:** hidden by default except a small bottom-centre control block (PREV UNIT, weapon name, NEXT UNIT,
  PRIME, turn counter, a time bar) and small MAIN MENU / GAME / RESTART buttons top-left, MODES bottom-left.
  A right-click context menu lists: Add Waypoint, Cancel Aim, Check, Engage on Sight, Continue on Sight, Stand, Duck,
  Focus on Enemy, Ignore Enemy, Clear Focus/Ignore.
* **Battle replay:** bright white tracer lines (thin, long, with a glow at the muzzle), big white-hot bloom for
  explosions with radial sparks and a shower of blue cube debris, red/green splatter, "NEXT TURN" big translucent
  title flash between turns, last-seen markers for unseen enemies ("LAST SEEN x s AGO").
* **Briefing screen (green):** a green monochrome satellite-style map with a 3D wireframe building, a thin white
  bracket marker and the mission title (e.g. "Ninstar Central Headquarters, Denshen - Defend"), a news ticker line, character
  portraits (briefing speakers), a dossier list and a START OPERATION button.
* **Tutorial popup:** small portrait + text box with a NEXT button, bottom right.

Planned (when the owner says go): restyle board_view.gd to this (light pools through openings, one-colour abstract
figures with volume, HUD pips, tracer/bloom/debris, bottom-centre PRIME block, NEXT TURN flash, green briefing screen).
